Update your schedule app

For overseers who set up their own copy of the schedule. Takes about 5 minutes.

Why you're seeing this

The schedule pages update for everyone automatically. A few new features also need the small program inside your own schedule sheet (its "server code") to be updated. Your data stays in your sheets; nothing is lost or moved.

1. Copy in the new server code

  1. Open your schedule sheet, then click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Open the new Code.gs in another tab. Select all of it (Ctrl+A or ⌘A) and copy it.
  3. Back in Apps Script, click Code.gs on the left, select everything there, and paste to replace it.
  4. Do the same with the new Index.html: copy it all, click Index.html on the left, select everything, and paste.
  5. Click the Save icon (💾).

2. Publish the update (keeps your same links)

  1. Click Deploy → Manage deployments.
  2. Click the pencil icon.
  3. Under Version, choose New version, then click Deploy.
  4. If Google asks you to approve access, choose your account, click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), check Select all, and click Continue. It says "unsafe" only because Google hasn't reviewed this homemade script.

Do not choose "New deployment"; that would give you a new link and the links you already sent would stop working.

3. Check it

Open your overseer page and refresh. The update notice should be gone.
